## Overview
The Halo studio light (halo-light) is a wide studio light for even lighting across a large desk or workspace.

## Light source
The Halo studio light gives wide, even illumination with a dimmer control.

## Repair & parts
The Halo studio light has serviceable components: the arm joints and the external power supply can be replaced.

Replacement arm joints and power supplies can be ordered from the manufacturer.

## Warranty
The Halo studio light has a two-year fixture warranty covering the arm, base and power supply.
