Product: Mini LED light

Note: Fictional demo data for a hackathon. Not a real product.

## Overview
The Mini LED light (mini-light) is a small portable light for travel and small desks.

## Light source
The Mini LED light uses a sealed LED design.

## Repair & parts
The Mini LED light bulb cannot be replaced; the LED is sealed inside the housing.

No parts are user-serviceable.

## Warranty
The Mini LED light has a one-year warranty. A failed LED is handled by replacing the whole light.
