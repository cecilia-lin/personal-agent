## Overview
The Arc desk light (arc-light) is a desk lamp with an adjustable arm for reading and desk work.

## Light source
The Arc desk light uses one standard E26 bulb.

## Repair & parts
The Arc desk light bulb is user-replaceable without tools.

To replace it, unplug the lamp, let the bulb cool, and unscrew it from the shade. Any standard E26 bulb fits.

## Warranty
The Arc desk light has a two-year fixture warranty. Bulbs are consumables and are not covered.
